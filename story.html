My story

A short version, in my own words, so you know exactly who you're voting for.

Where I'm from

I was born in Las Piñas, Philippines. I moved to Columbus, Ohio, where I currently live and love!

How I got to UChicago

Ever since looking into UChicago when my friend (UChicago class of 2027) got into UChicago, I have been in love with the school. I love the environment of discussion, debate, and pursuit of knowledge. I wouldn't trade this opportunity for the world.

What I'm doing here

I am currently a Global Voices Metcalf Intern at International House. Come to some of our events through the lecture series and cultural events! You are in for a treat.

Current Life Trajectory

  1. late 2007-late 2011Moved to Ohio from the Philippines
  2. Lived in a Small Rural Fishing Village called Indian Lake (shoutout Czar D. class of '29)
  3. High School at Columbus Academy in Gahanna, Ohio
  4. 2026College with you all!

Why I'm running

I'm running on three priorities: community, access, and transparency. I believe that our school is an amazing community and environment for scholarship. I want to act as a voice for our class to ensure that our needs are met and to improve the college experience for everyone with pragmatic solutions!

What you should know about me

You deserve a straight picture of who is asking for your vote. I played soccer and helped lead multiple clubs in High School: Health Occupations Students of America, Music Performance Club, Model United Nations. Throughout my time in High School, I made many lifelong friends, promoted cultural diversity through cultural groups I was a part of, organized alumni events and worked closely with administration to improve the experience of my classmates. I also advocated for climate justice and youth quality of life!
