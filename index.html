Three priorities, each with a plan.

Every proposal states what Student Government can do directly, who I'll work with, and how you'll know I delivered.

Community

Community life at UChicago is crucial to our experience as college students. Some areas of community still need to be improved.

  • Improve & Expand Third Spaces
  • Increase 2 UChicago Dorm Guest limit to 4
  • Done by: Spring Quarter

Access

On many fronts, students face issues with access to basic necessities. Let's work to make campus more accessible.

  • Introduce more robust and accessible financial assistance programs for printing and laundry.
  • Extend maroon dollars to be used for laundry and printing.
  • Include menstrual products in dorm restrooms as a part of wellness needs.
  • Done by: Spring Quarter

Transparency

Where does tuition money go? How is USG & the University spending their money?

  • Introduce more in-depth online tuition breakdown
  • Provide receipts for Student Government Finance Committee purchases
  • Done by: Spring Quarter
